1. What the App does

MatterTracker helps you follow your matters before the courts and tribunals it supports. It retrieves publicly available court information — cause lists, live display boards, case records and orders — matches it against the matters you choose to track, estimates how many matters away your case is, and sends you alerts you have configured. Where available, it may also show photographs of unofficial cause lists displayed outside individual courtrooms and use them to estimate the order in which matters may be taken up.
