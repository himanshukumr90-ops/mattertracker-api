Privacy Policy

MatterTracker  ·  Last updated: 1 July 2026

MatterTracker (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a case-tracking tool for advocates and litigants. It notifies you when your matters are listed before courts and tribunals and how close they are to being called. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account and using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

This policy is operated by Himanshu. For any privacy question or request, contact us at mattertracker0@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and an authentication credential, used to create and secure your account.
  • Case information you provide. The case details you add to track a matter — case type, number and year, and any notes — and the preferences you set (such as notification thresholds and per-court alert settings).
  • Court record information. To provide the service, the App retrieves publicly available information published by the courts and tribunals it supports (such as cause lists, display boards, and case status or orders) and associates it with the matters you track — for example the court or bench, item/serial number, hearing dates, party names and advocate names as published.
  • Notification & device information. If you enable push notifications, we store a push subscription token for your device so that alerts can be delivered to it. We also keep a log of notifications generated for your matters.
  • Usage information. Basic information about which matters and courts you track, used to operate and improve the App.

We do not collect payment information (the App is free), and we do not request location, contacts, photos, or other device data.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: tracking your matters, matching them to published cause lists, calculating how many matters away your case is, and sending you alerts.
  • To deliver notifications you have asked for (for example, when your case is a set number of items away, when a court begins taking passovers, or when your matter is called).
  • To maintain, secure, debug and improve the App.
  • To communicate with you about your account or important service changes.

4. Court data and public records

Cause lists, display-board status, and case details shown in the App originate from the publicly accessible systems of the courts and tribunals the App supports. This is public court-record information and is presented to help you track your matters. The App is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of any court, tribunal, or government body.

5. Push notifications

Push notifications are optional. If you enable them, we store a device push token to deliver alerts. You can turn notifications off at any time from within the App or from your device settings; doing so stops further alerts to that device.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • We retain your account and case data for as long as your account is active.
  • You can stop tracking and delete individual matters at any time within the App.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by using the in-app deletion option (where available) or by contacting us at mattertracker0@gmail.com. We will delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the App and our services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

MatterTracker is a professional tool intended for advocates, litigants and legal professionals. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

9. International users

The App is operated for users in India and primarily concerns courts and tribunals in India. Information may be processed and stored by our service providers on infrastructure located in India or other countries. By using the App you consent to such processing.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above, and significant changes may be communicated within the App. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
